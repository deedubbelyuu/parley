    macOS isn’t letting Parley record the screen.

    After an update, macOS forgets that it trusted Parley, even though the switch in System Settings still looks on. Parley can make macOS ask again.

    1. Press Fix it. If macOS shows a box about screen recording, choose Allow (or Open System Settings and switch Parley on).
    2. Press Restart Parley. Your call ends; just call back.
